# Plainbook workflow comparison

Reference inspected in the local source checkout at commit `d2ab76be6fdcf26d08ab676f916ff12749ec3936` (Plainbook 1.15.0). The comparison uses the application handlers, models, balance calculations, statistics, reports, guide and tests. ## Workflows in this release

| Reference area       | Liquidation behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Journal and accounts | Compact five-metric dashboard, account balance tiles, single-line open/closed trade ledgers, period grouping, clickable detail dialogs, full-page trade editors, entry and exit image evidence, and edit/close/BE actions. Preparation, thoughts, watchlists, missed trades and calendar remain available.                                                                                                    |
| Accounts and money   | Stable trading accounts, broker/prop/demo type, opening balance, account attribution, history, deposits, withdrawals, fees, corrections and archive. The first account chooses a shared journal currency. Legacy labels can be adopted.                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Prop-firm rules      | Daily loss, closed/open-risk switches, static and trailing loss floors, profit target, minimum entry days and firm timezone/reset. Values use recorded closed results and open stop risk.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Plans                | Compact active/past table, overall worked/all-plans rate, reviewed-plan accuracy and separate realized plan win rate, status and playbook filters, direct recoverable deletion, linked trades, versioned evidence, reviews and potential results.                                                                                                                                                             |
| Playbooks            | Compact table, editable/versioned rules, checklists, examples and review progress. Every template supports overview and setup images. Standard, strategy and community templates are available.                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| DRC/WRC              | Quarter calendar with daily and weekly grade, P&L, trade counts and review state. DRC uses closing dates, WRC uses entry weeks. Saved cards include text, assessments, screenshots and optional written P&L.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Statistics           | Twelve overview tables, grouped filter dropdowns, one detailed factor selector, shared filters and sortable groups with independent multi-row subtotals, month-of-year aggregation, deterministic insights and historical inclusion/exclusion tests, plus cumulative money/R curves, R distribution, drawdown, period and price evidence, stop overruns, BE flag, checklist results and selection comparison. |
| Mistakes             | Selectable and custom trade mistakes, critical annotations, review coverage, recurrence, monthly/daily/weekday trends, latest versus previous reviewed samples and linked trades.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Guide                | Bilingual in-app workflow guide, factor definitions and metric glossary. Informational tooltips identify filter and table behavior.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Reports              | History of months/quarters with recorded closes or saved reviews and the current period, reviewed-day coverage, prior-period comparison, best/worst measured trade, account breakdown and shared analytics.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Deletion             | Recoverable Trash and confirmed permanent deletion of records and unreferenced screenshots. Referential checks preserve associated history.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

## Remaining differences

| Area               | Remaining capability                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Accounting         | Different native currencies in one journal with explicit conversion or currency-separated aggregate results, and synchronized copies of one trading idea across accounts.                                                            |
| Trade record       | Full financial-record editing of a closed trade, intermediate trade update timelines and additional execution fields. Entry and exit evidence, review notes, context and optional price evidence can be edited from the review form. |
| Form lists         | Owner-managed catalogue of allowed instruments, types, timeframes and styles. Trading styles currently use editable text and suggestions from history.                                                                               |
| Review cards       | Moving a saved card to a different date and freely written assessment subjects or outcome overrides.                                                                                                                                 |
| Reports            | Export/share of generated reports, all empty periods between historical periods, and a year heatmap spanning every day.                                                                                                              |
| Journal management | Cross-record full text search, managed backup import/export, archive restore preview, and configurable simplified mode.                                                                                                              |

Liquidation retains its additional backtests, missed-opportunity scenarios, three playbook templates, Watchlist, daily thoughts, lectures, curated best trades, recent-trade review and local AI Review. The implementation uses its own React/TypeScript modules and styling.

The statistics overview also follows the factor-by-factor structure in the public [Forex Dashboard reference](https://app.notion.com/p/tonyjs/Forex-Dashboard-b9a8362b90284c0a98cf031ad8def46f), with the journal’s own account definitions, trades, classification labels and styling.

The reference includes multiple accounting and review conventions. Liquidation uses positive net P&L divided by all closed trades for win rate, initial recorded monetary risk for realized R, and a single journal currency. The README documents the current calculation rules.
